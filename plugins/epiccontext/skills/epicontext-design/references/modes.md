# Modes — generate, critique, choose

Generate and critique are the same loop entered at different points; a
critique ends with a redesign proposal and a generation ends with a
self-critique.

## Generate — "design the X screen", "give me directions", "ontwerp"

1. Steps 0–2: style cards, intake, project rules. Missing rules or
   style card → propose first.
2. Produce two or three directions that embody **different bets**
   (density vs breathing room, familiarity vs distinctiveness), never one
   direction in three colours. State each bet as *A even over B* and say
   what it gives up. Each direction names the style-card elements it
   uses.
3. Only tokens and components from the graph (P4, P9); a token the design
   needs but the scale lacks is a finding, not an invention.
4. **Self-critique before presenting**: steps 3–7 on your own directions;
   fix severity ≥3. Then: where does this look like the default I'd
   produce for any similar brief (the three AI clusters in
   `references/red-flags.md`)? Revise that part and say what changed.
5. The loud moment (P5), if any, is named, justified in one sentence, and
   has its reduced-motion twin.

Canvas and artboard mechanics are in `references/canvas-and-brandbook.md`;
the loop decides what good looks like, not how the canvas is driven.

## Critique — "is this good?", "beoordeel dit"

Run steps 1–9. Output exactly the template in
`references/critique-template.md`: context and assumptions, measurements,
three lenses as tables (**at most eight findings per lens**; the rest
counts in the score but stays out of the table), drift, what works and
must stay, uncertainty, verdict with top-3 actions and the redesign
proposal for round two. Prioritise the failures in
`references/red-flags.md`. Every finding names a floor item, a principle
number or a standard with its evidence label, plus its evidence. Never
"feels off".

## Choose — "which variant?", including `/design` artboards

Measure and floor every option first; an option failing severity ≥3
drops out before taste enters. Judge the survivors on the principles in
the light of the recorded stand. Name the bet each option makes,
recommend one, and state the condition under which you would reverse the
choice.

## Case — "kijk naar het geheel", "werk dit uit", a review with people

When the question is the whole rather than one screen — a flow across
screens, a direction that changes many places, a critique to talk through
with the people who decide — offer to lay it out on one page, and wait for
a yes: *"Zal ik dit als case uitwerken in Figma, met alle context erbij?"*
What a case holds, how to make it and how it comes back to the graph:
`references/figma-case.md`.
