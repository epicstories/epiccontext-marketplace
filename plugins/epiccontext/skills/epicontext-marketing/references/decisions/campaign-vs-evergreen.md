# Decision — Campaign frame vs evergreen content

**The decision:** does a content piece deserve a `campaign` frame (a goal, a window, a hypothesis), or is it evergreen?

**How to decide:**
- **Campaign** when there's a time-bound outcome to move (a launch, a season, a push on a priority CEP) and you'll measure KPI movement in a window. Wire `campaign_ref` → `strategy_ref`.
- **Evergreen** when the piece occupies a durable Category Entry Point and earns compounding search/AI presence over time (the classic answer-first SEO/GEO page). Mark it evergreen with a `notes:` explaining the CEP it owns — don't leave it orphaned.

**Rule of thumb:** a CEP page that should rank and get cited for years is evergreen; a dated push to hit a number is a campaign. Both are legitimate; orphaned-with-no-reason is not.

**Trade-off:** campaign framing adds measurement discipline but a window; evergreen compounds but is harder to attribute. Don't force a window onto content whose value is long-tail CEP coverage.

**Reversal condition:** promote evergreen content into a campaign when you want a measured push behind it; retire a campaign's pieces to evergreen once the window closes and they still serve a CEP.
