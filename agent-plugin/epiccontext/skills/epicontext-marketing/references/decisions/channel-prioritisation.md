# Decision — Channel prioritisation (pick 2–4)

**The decision:** which channels get real investment this period, and which stay dormant or become anti-strategy.

**Why it's hard:** every channel looks plausible; spreading across all of them means winning none. Mental availability is built by showing up *consistently* where the persona is — depth beats breadth on channels (the opposite of CEPs, where breadth wins).

**How to decide:**
1. **Reach** — does the channel actually reach a priority `persona` (check the persona's `where_they_hang_out`)? Drop channels that don't.
2. **CEP fit** — does the channel let you show up at priority Category Entry Points? (e.g. organic/AI search occupies "actively looking" CEPs; community occupies "asking peers" CEPs.)
3. **Credibility & cost** — can you sustain the cadence at a believable CAC?
4. Pick **2–4**. Name the rest explicitly as dormant (revisit later) or anti-strategy (deliberately not doing).

**Trade-off to state honestly:** concentrating on 2–4 channels means conceding presence elsewhere. That's correct — partial presence everywhere builds no memory link anywhere.

**Reversal condition:** revisit when a priority CEP is under-served by the current channel set, or a channel misses its CAC target over a full cycle (not one bad week).
